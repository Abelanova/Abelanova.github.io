I am an HCI researcher and MSc student in Product-Service System Design at Politecnico di Milano. My research brings together **human–AI interaction, immersive computing, spatial interaction, and multimodal interfaces**. I am particularly interested in how intelligent and embodied systems can make complex information easier to perceive, understand, and act on.

My work spans virtual and physical interaction: immersive visualization with 3D Gaussian Splatting, responsive desk objects for biofeedback, vision-to-tactile learning, accessible AI agents, and VR tools for tactical sensemaking. I combine system building and interaction design with qualitative and quantitative research methods.

I am currently interested in PhD opportunities and research collaborations in HCI, XR, and human-centered AI.

Research interests
======

- Human–AI interaction
- Immersive computing and AR/VR
- Spatial interaction and visual perception
- Multimodal and embodied interfaces
- Accessibility and inclusive design

Selected research
======

**Interactive Exploration of Camera Distortions using 3D Gaussian Splats in VR**  
Université Paris-Saclay, LISN · IEEE ISMAR 2026 Demo  
Developed a Unity prototype for controllable wide-field-of-view and fisheye exploration of 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes in stereo VR. [Official demo listing](https://www.ieeeismar.net/2026/demos/) · [Code](https://github.com/Abelanova/3dgs-vr-fisheye-prototype)

**Responsive Desk Objects for Workplace Wellbeing and Performance**  
Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems · RIGI 2026  
Created embodied biofeedback prototypes using pneumatic actuation, granular jamming, movement, and vibration, and co-designed feedback concepts with 13 participants.

**Vision-to-Tactile Learning for Multimodal Haptic Rendering**  
Hefei University of Technology · MAC Lab  
Explored RGB-based material-property inference and tactile representations using PyTorch and GelSight tactile images.
