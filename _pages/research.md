My research investigates how intelligent, immersive, and embodied interfaces can support perception, learning, and decision-making. I work across system building, interaction design, physical prototyping, and empirical evaluation.

Interactive Exploration of Camera Distortions using 3D Gaussian Splats in VR
======

**Université Paris-Saclay, LISN, ARAI Team · 2026**

Conventional perspective projection can limit peripheral context in immersive 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes. I implemented controllable wide-FOV and fisheye rendering in Unity, exploring how nonlinear projection transforms Gaussian centers and screen-space footprints while preserving per-eye visibility and binocular fusion.

**Methods and tools:** 3D Gaussian Splatting, Unity, C#, stereo VR, fisheye rendering  
[IEEE ISMAR 2026 Demo](https://www.ieeeismar.net/2026/demos/) · [Prototype repository](https://github.com/Abelanova/3dgs-vr-fisheye-prototype)

Responsive Desk Objects for Workplace Wellbeing and Performance
======

**Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems · Robotics Institute Germany Internships · 2026**

This project investigated subtle mechanical biofeedback embedded in familiar desk objects. I developed modular prototypes using pneumatic actuation, granular jamming, servo-driven movement, and vibration, and co-designed feedback concepts with 13 participants.

**Methods and tools:** physical prototyping, biofeedback, co-design, interoception, Arduino

Vision-to-Tactile Learning for Multimodal Haptic Rendering
======

**Hefei University of Technology, MAC Lab · 2025–2026**

This project explores how RGB images can inform material-property inference and tactile representations in AR/VR. I developed a supervised PyTorch baseline and a complementary GelSight tactile-image generation pipeline.

**Methods and tools:** PyTorch, GelSight, computer vision, haptics, AR/VR

TactiCastVR
======

**VR sensemaking · Manuscript in preparation**

TactiCastVR supports first-person soccer tactical analysis in VR through formation cues, event indicators, viewpoint recommendations, and temporal traces. A controlled study with 45 participants found improved tactical understanding and reduced workload compared with unassisted VR.

**Methods and tools:** sports HCI, VR, sensemaking, controlled user study

Intelligent Agent for Accessible Web Navigation
======

**Politecnico di Milano, HINT Lab · 2025**

A voice navigation agent for blind and low-vision users, combining speech interaction, browser automation, and accessibility metadata. I helped design six interactive prototypes and contributed to usability evaluations.

**Methods and tools:** accessibility, voice interaction, browser agents, prototyping
