<p class="section-intro">My research lies at the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction, extended reality, embodied interfaces, and multimodal interaction. I develop working systems and study how people perceive, interact with, and make sense of information in virtual and physical environments.</p>

<p>The project visuals below are drawn from my <em>PhD Application Presentation</em> (October 2026), except for the explicitly labelled conceptual diagram for vision-to-tactile learning.</p>

    <h2>Interactive Exploration of Camera Distortions using 3D Gaussian Splats in VR</h2>
    <p class="research-meta">Université Paris-Saclay · LISN / ARAI · May–July 2026 · IEEE ISMAR 2026 Demonstration</p>
    <p>Conventional perspective projection limits peripheral visual context when viewing 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes. I built a stereoscopic Unity prototype for interactive wide-field-of-view and fisheye projection, investigating how to transform both Gaussian centers and screen-space covariance while maintaining per-eye visibility.</p>
    <p><strong>My contribution:</strong> VR implementation in Unity and C#, cubemap resampling, direct Gaussian projection, and rendering comparisons.</p>
    <p class="research-tags">3DGS · VR · Unity · OpenXR · Computer Graphics · Wide-FOV</p>

    <h2>Embodied Work: Responsive Desk Objects for Workplace Wellbeing and Performance</h2>
    <p class="research-meta">Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems · RIGI 2026 · July–September 2026</p>
    <p>We explored whether subtle, tangible biofeedback integrated into familiar workplace objects can support interoceptive awareness and reflection. The work considered breathing, heart rate and variability, electrodermal activity, and muscle tension as input for physical feedback.</p>
    <p><strong>My contribution:</strong> Developed and explored pneumatic, granular-jamming, servo-driven, and vibrotactile prototypes; participated in system design and co-design workshops with 13 participants.</p>
    <p class="research-tags">Haptics · Biofeedback · Physical Prototyping · Co-design · Interoception</p>

    <h2>TactiCastVR: First-Person Soccer Tactic Sensemaking in VR</h2>
    <p class="research-meta">Immersive sports visualization · Manuscript in preparation</p>
    <p>To help viewers understand dynamic soccer tactics from an immersive first-person perspective, TactiCastVR integrates formation cues, event indicators, viewpoint recommendations, and temporal traces. A controlled study with 45 participants found improved tactical understanding and reduced workload compared with unassisted VR.</p>
    <p><strong>My contribution:</strong> Led Unity/C# VR system development, XR interaction and deployment, and the controlled user study.</p>
    <p class="research-tags">VR · Immersive Analytics · Sports HCI · User Study (N = 45)</p>
  </div>
</article>

  <div>
    <h2>Vision-to-Tactile Learning for Multimodal Haptic Rendering</h2>
    <p class="research-meta">Hefei University of Technology · MAC Lab · December 2025–March 2026</p>
    <p>This ongoing research direction examines how visual signals can inform physical material-property prediction and tactile-image generation for immersive interfaces. It explores approaches to aligning visual and tactile representations rather than relying exclusively on direct tactile sensing.</p>
    <p><strong>My contribution:</strong> Developed a supervised baseline in PyTorch, experimented with a GelSight tactile-image pipeline, and explored cross-modal learning and evaluation strategies.</p>
    <p class="research-tags">Computer Vision · PyTorch · GelSight · Haptic Rendering · Cross-modal Learning</p>
