<p class="page-intro">I study how intelligent, immersive, and embodied interfaces can make information easier to perceive and act on. My work moves between system building, interaction design, and empirical evaluation.</p>

<div class="research-projects">
  <article class="project-card project-card--featured">
    <div class="project-kicker">IMMERSIVE COMPUTING · UNIVERSITÉ PARIS-SACLAY</div>
    <h2>Interactive Exploration of Camera Distortions using 3D Gaussian Splats in VR</h2>
    <p>Conventional perspective projection limits peripheral context in immersive 3DGS scenes. I implemented controllable wide-FOV and fisheye rendering in Unity, exploring how nonlinear projection transforms Gaussian centers and screen-space footprints while preserving per-eye visibility and binocular fusion.</p>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>3D Gaussian Splatting</span><span>Unity</span><span>Stereo VR</span><span>Fisheye Rendering</span></div>

  <article class="project-card">
    <div class="project-kicker">EMBODIED INTERACTION · MAX PLANCK INSTITUTE FOR INTELLIGENT SYSTEMS</div>
    <h2>Responsive Desk Objects for Workplace Wellbeing and Performance</h2>
    <p>At MPI-IS, I investigated subtle mechanical biofeedback embedded in everyday desk objects. I developed modular prototypes using pneumatic actuation, granular jamming, servo-driven movement, and vibration, and co-designed feedback concepts with 13 participants.</p>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>Biofeedback</span><span>Physical Prototyping</span><span>Co-design</span><span>Interoception</span></div>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <div class="project-kicker">MULTIMODAL LEARNING · HEFEI UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY</div>
    <h2>Vision-to-Tactile Learning for Multimodal Haptic Rendering</h2>
    <p>This project explores how RGB images can inform material-property inference and tactile representations in AR/VR. I developed a supervised PyTorch baseline and a complementary GelSight tactile-image generation pipeline.</p>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>PyTorch</span><span>GelSight</span><span>Haptics</span><span>AR/VR</span></div>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <div class="project-kicker">VR SENSEMAKING · MANUSCRIPT IN PREPARATION</div>
    <h2>TactiCastVR</h2>
    <p>TactiCastVR supports first-person soccer tactical analysis in VR through formation cues, event indicators, viewpoint recommendations, and temporal traces. A controlled study with 45 participants found improved tactical understanding and reduced workload compared with unassisted VR.</p>
    <div class="tag-list"><span>Sports HCI</span><span>VR</span><span>Sensemaking</span><span>Empirical Study</span></div>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <div class="project-kicker">ACCESSIBILITY · POLITECNICO DI MILANO</div>
